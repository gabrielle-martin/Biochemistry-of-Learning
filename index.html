Key Takeaway:

Deep learning occurs when students experience:
  • Safety and energy (Endorphins)
  • Momentum and success (Dopamine)
  • Belonging and trust (Serotonin)
  • Challenge and productive struggle (Cortisol)
The goal is not maximizing one hormone — it is creating balance across all four.
